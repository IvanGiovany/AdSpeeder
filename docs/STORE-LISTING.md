# Store listing draft — review before submission

**Name:** AdSpeeder

**Short description:** Speed through video ads, mute them, and return to your playback settings.

AdSpeeder watches for ads on supported video pages. When an ad appears, it uses the highest
supported speed up to your selected limit and optionally mutes it. When the ad ends, it restores
your previous speed and mute state. Live streams use mute only.

- Choose 2×, 4×, 8× or 16×; actual speed depends on your browser and the player.
- Enable or pause the extension instantly; choose platforms individually.
- See local daily and all-time statistics. No telemetry or account required.
- Blocky pixel-art interface in light and dark, with keyboard controls and reduced-motion support.

YouTube is supported. Netflix is **Experimental**, disabled by default, and its selectors
are currently unverified. Do not claim Netflix compatibility until real-account validation.
AdSpeeder does not block network requests, remove ads or click Skip. Platform changes can
break detection. Review each store's current policies and platform terms before publishing.
